# Code of conduct

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help.

Not welcome here: personal attacks, harassment, or dismissing someone for what
they do not know yet. A bug report written badly is still a bug report.

Issues and pull requests are about the content. Keep them about the content.

If something crosses a line, email `rick@agentmore.app`. Reports are handled
privately, and the maintainers may edit, lock or remove contributions and block
accounts that do not follow this.
